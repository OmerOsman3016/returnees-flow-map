Overview

UNHCR · End 2024

Sudan hosted 837,966 refugees and asylum-seekers at end of 2024 — a 13% decline from 2023, driven by secondary displacement after the April 2023 SAF–RSF conflict. Before the conflict, Sudan was Africa's 2nd-largest refugee host, sheltering over 1 million people. South Sudanese remain the largest group, followed by Eritreans and Ethiopians.
